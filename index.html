Cold call script — motivated sellers
Hi, is this [Owner's name]? Hi [Name], my name is Ibrahim — I'm a local real estate investor here in DFW. I buy houses directly from homeowners, as-is, cash, and I can close fast — usually 2–3 weeks. I came across your property at [address] and wanted to reach out — is that something you'd consider selling? — If yes — Great. I'm not a realtor, so there are no commissions or fees. I make a cash offer, you pick the closing date, we handle everything. Can I ask — what's your situation with the property? Are you currently living there? Listen — motivated sellers will tell you everything. Don't interrupt. And if I could make you a fair cash offer today, what would you need to get out of it? They give a number. Don't react. Pause. Then say: I appreciate that. Let me run some numbers on my end and I'll get back to you with an offer by [tomorrow / end of week]. Would that work? Get their number and email before hanging up. Always confirm the best time to call back.
Objection responses
"I'm not interested"
"No problem at all — can I ask, is it because you're happy with the property or already have plans for it?" (often opens the door)
"I already have a realtor"
"That's great — I work differently. No listing, no showings, no commissions. If the timing ever changes, keep my number."
"What's your offer?"
"I need to see the property first to be fair to you. When's a good time for a quick 15-min walkthrough?"
"I want full price"
"I totally understand — the trade-off is speed and certainty. No repairs, no waiting. Would getting all cash in 2 weeks work at a slightly lower price?"
"I need to think about it"
"Of course — this is a big decision. Can I follow up on [specific day]? I want to make sure you have all the info."
"My neighbor said I could get more"
"They may be right on list price — the difference is I'm offering cash, no repairs, no contingencies, and closing on your timeline. That has real value."
